## 🚀 Executive Summary

With a decade of enterprise experience architecting and deploying production-grade AI/ML systems, I specialize in translating complex business challenges into scalable, high-throughput machine learning and Generative AI platforms. 

Currently at **Constellation Energy**, designing mission-critical modern sales intelligence platforms, automated real-time fraud mitigation architectures, and resilient multi-agent RAG pipelines.
